# AI and Modern Indexing Enrichment

Add a clearly labelled supplementary unit explaining how modern AI can support, not automatically replace, professional indexing and abstracting judgment.

Cover:
- automatic keyword extraction
- classification assistance
- named-entity recognition
- summarization
- semantic search
- retrieval-augmented generation at a conceptual level
- human review and correction
- hallucination detection
- source provenance
- bias and representativeness
- privacy and confidential documents
- copyright considerations
- evaluation of AI-generated indexes and abstracts

Include practical activities where learners compare human-created and AI-assisted outputs using transparent criteria. Avoid claiming that AI outputs are inherently correct.
