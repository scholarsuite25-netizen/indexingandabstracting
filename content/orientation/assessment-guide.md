## Assessment and Examination Guide

### Assessment

| Assessment | Weight | Main Preparation |
|---|---|---|
| Indexing Practicum | 20% | Thesaurus entry design, book-index construction, and PRECIS exercises |
| Abstracting Portfolio | 15% | Indicative, informative, and critical abstracts |
| Mid-Term Assessment | 15% | Pre-coordinate versus post-coordinate analysis |
| Final Examination | 50% | Comprehensive theory and practical examination, including digital and AI-assisted indexing |

### Examination rules

**Objective Examination**

- Time Allowed: 1 Hour 30 Minutes
- This paper contains ONE HUNDRED (100) objective questions.
- Answer ALL questions.
- Each question has four options, A to D. Only ONE option is correct.
- Circle or shade the letter of the correct option on your answer sheet.
- Each question carries one (1) mark.

**Theory Examination**

- Time Allowed: 2 Hours
- This paper contains SEVEN (7) questions.
- Answer any FIVE (5) questions only.
- All questions carry equal marks (20 marks each).
- Write clearly and number your answers to match the question numbers on this paper.

## LMS defaults (not from the source)

These are configurable defaults chosen by this LMS build. They do **not** come from the study guide
or the examination papers, and they may be changed by course staff:

- Theory pass mark: **50 / 100**
- Objective pass mark: **50**
- Knowledge-check pass: **70**
- Theory unlock requires **70%** on the objective paper
- Reading threshold: **90%** of a lesson must be read before completion
- Attempts: **unlimited**, with the **best** attempt counting
