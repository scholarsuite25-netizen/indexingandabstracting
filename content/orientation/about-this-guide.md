## Copyright and Use

This study guide is an original educational compilation prepared to support students taking LIS 815. It draws on the LIS 815 and LIS 814 course outlines and on standard reference works in the field, and is intended for use as a study aid alongside prescribed texts and lecture materials. Where this guide differs from the instructions of a lecturer or the requirements of an institution, those instructions and requirements take precedence.

This expanded edition incorporates additional material on book indexing, the digital and AI-assisted transformation of indexing practice, and further scholarly definitions and citations, while preserving the structure, tone and pedagogical approach of the original edition.

## Preface

This book has been written for postgraduate students who are new to the subject of indexing and abstracting. Plain language is used throughout, while retaining the technical terms required for the course.

The guide follows a fourteen-chapter structure organised into seven modules, and places emphasis on understanding before memorisation. Each chapter includes learning objectives, explanations in everyday language, worked examples, and review questions to help consolidate learning.

For systems such as PRECIS, students should follow the exact operator rules prescribed by their lecturer or course manual, since notational conventions can vary between institutions.

The structure of this guide reflects common assessment components in courses of this kind: thesaurus construction and PRECIS practice, abstract writing, comparison of pre-coordinate and post-coordinate systems, and retrieval calculations.
