## Final Revision Checklist

- [ ] Explain the major terms of this course in your own words.
- [ ] Cite at least one scholarly definition of indexing and of an index (e.g. Lancaster, Mulvany, Knight, or the relevant ISO standard).
- [ ] Construct basic thesaurus relationships (BT, NT, RT, USE, UF).
- [ ] Distinguish clearly between pre-coordinate, post-coordinate, and derived indexing.
- [ ] Explain PRECIS, its origin, its syntax, and its role operators.
- [ ] Construct simple KWIC and KWOC displays.
- [ ] Describe the components and types of a book index, and the stages of book indexing.
- [ ] Calculate precision, recall, and fallout.
- [ ] Explain exhaustivity, specificity, and the other criteria used to evaluate an index.
- [ ] Write indicative, informative, and critical abstracts.
- [ ] Compare human and automated approaches to indexing and abstracting, including the role of AI.
- [ ] Answer practical questions in your own words, without relying on memorised wording.
