## Opening the course

Sign in and open LIS 815 Indexing and Abstracting from your dashboard. The Start Here pages — the
ones you are reading now — come first; the seven modules follow.

## Working through lessons

Lessons unlock in sequence: a lesson becomes available once the earlier lessons are complete, so
the course guides you forward one step at a time. Work through them in order rather than trying to
jump ahead.

The reader tracks how much of a lesson you have read. **Reading progress must reach the 90%
threshold before "Mark as completed" will work** — scroll through the whole lesson, then mark it
complete. If the button will not respond, that is why.

## Knowledge checks

Each chapter ends with a knowledge check that gates the chapter. Attempt it once you have read the
chapter; the explanations are there to teach you, so read them even for questions you answered
correctly.

## Picking up where you left off

Your progress is saved. Close the tab and come back later and the course will return you to where
you stopped — there is no need to restart a lesson you have already begun.

## If the course says "not enrolled"

If a course shows as not enrolled, enrol from the course page or your dashboard first; the content
opens once enrolment is recorded. If it still will not open, ask for help.

## Getting help

Help lives at `/help`. Use it if something in the reader does not behave as described on this page.
