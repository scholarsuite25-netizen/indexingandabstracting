# LIS 815 — Enrichment Scope

The supplied materials are the authoritative core course source. The following are supplementary enrichment and should be labelled as such.

## Additional useful topics
- Authority control and authority files
- Subject headings and controlled vocabularies
- Faceted classification and faceted search
- MARC and bibliographic metadata concepts
- Dublin Core and repository metadata
- Citation indexing
- Full-text indexing
- Inverted indexes and search-engine basics
- Stemming and lemmatization
- Named-entity recognition for information organization
- Natural-language processing for indexing
- Semantic search and embeddings at a conceptual level
- Information retrieval evaluation beyond precision and recall
- User studies and usability evaluation
- Search logs and query analysis
- Accessibility and inclusive information retrieval
- Digital repositories and scholarly communication
- Open educational resources and metadata
- AI-assisted indexing and abstracting
- Human-in-the-loop AI workflows
- Hallucination, bias, provenance and verification
- Copyright and responsible use of source documents
- Privacy and data protection in learner systems
- Nigerian/African library and information-service applications
- Practical case studies and mini-projects

## Learning design
Every enrichment item should state that it is supplementary. Do not present enrichment as a quotation or direct teaching from the supplied LIS 815 materials.
